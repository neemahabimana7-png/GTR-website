ABOUT GRITTECH RESOURCES Limited

Grittech Resources Ltd is committed to delivering responsible mineral trading solutions by connecting African resources with global markets through transparency, integrity and operational excellence.

WHO WE ARE

Building Trusted Mineral Trading Across Africa

Grittech Resources Ltd is a leading mineral trading company specializing in sourcing, trading and exporting valuable minerals including Gold, Copper, Coltan, Tantalum, Tin, Tungsten and Manganese. Our commitment to transparency, responsible sourcing, strategic partnerships and international standards enables us to create sustainable value for our clients, communities and partners.
