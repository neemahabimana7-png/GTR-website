WHO WE ARE

A Trusted Partner in Mineral Trading

Grittech Resources Ltd is an investment company incorporated in Rwanda, with its core business being mineral trading.

We trade a wide range of ferrous and non-ferrous metals, catering to the specific metal needs of our customers. We expand our sourcing through strategic alliances with key stakeholders, supported by transparent traceability and KYC processes.

Our commercial operations focus on sourcing different grades of metals and facilitating mineral buying and selling,with pricing based on London Metal Exchange (LME) rates for registered commodities.
