OUR CORE MINERAL PORTFOLIO

Gold (Au)

A precious metal widely used in jewelry, investment, electronics, and industry.

Tin (Sn)

Extracted from cassiterite and used in soldering, electronics, and metal coatings.

Tantalum (Ta)

Obtained from coltan and used in electronic capacitors, smartphones, and aerospace components.

Tungsten (W)

Extracted from wolframite and used in cutting tools, heavy machinery, and high-temperature applications.
